THE DETAILS

Good to know.

Can I rent directly from Desert Lakes?

At this time, rentals are arranged through Vast.ai. This website introduces our hosting business; it does not take orders or process payments.

Where do I find specifications and pricing?

Check the individual Vast.ai listing for current hardware specifications, hourly rates, additional charges, and availability. We do not publish fixed rental rates here.

How do I get help with a rental?

Use the support options associated with your rental in your Vast.ai account. For account, billing, cancellation, or refund questions, consult Vast.ai’s support and the terms applicable to your rental.

Does this website collect my information?

This site has no contact form, checkout, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process standard technical information when serving the website. When you follow a link to Vast.ai, its privacy policy and terms apply.
